Cookie Policy

Transparent guidelines on how we utilize cookies to enhance your luxury property browsing experience

1. Introduction

Welcome to Realhouse Estate. This Cookie Policy explains how we use cookies and similar technologies to recognize, track, and save your preferences when you visit our website. We believe in being transparent about how we collect and process your data to deliver a premier property browsing experience.

By continuing to use our website, you agree to our use of cookies as described in this policy. You can manage your preferences at any time using your web browser settings.

2. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are stored on your computer or mobile device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work more efficiently, improve page performance, and provide crucial browsing state information to the website owners.

Cookies can be "Session Cookies" (which are deleted automatically when you close your browser) or "Persistent Cookies" (which remain on your device for a pre-set period or until you delete them manually).

3. How Realhouse Estate Uses Cookies

We use cookies and equivalent browser storage mechanisms (such as localStorage) to optimize your interaction with our luxury real estate showcase. These serve several vital purposes:

  • Essential Functionality: To remember your interactions, such as properties you add to your Wishlist, so they remain saved when you return or refresh the page.
  • Privacy Management: To remember whether you have accepted or declined our GDPR Cookie Consent Banner so that we do not repeatedly prompt you on subsequent visits.
  • Performance & Personalization: To remember user preferences such as your active search filters (Buy vs. Rent, location inputs, and price brackets).

4. Cookies & Local Storage We Set

Below is a detailed breakdown of the functional cookies and browser storage identifiers utilized on our platform:

Identifier Type Duration Purpose
realhouse_wishlist Local Storage Persistent Stores the unique IDs of properties you have "hearted" (saved to your favorites) so they persist across browser sessions.
cookie_consent_accepted Local Storage 1 Year Stored when you click "Accept" on our privacy banner, preventing the banner from showing on future visits.
cookie_consent_declined Local Storage Session Stored when you click "Decline" on our privacy banner, suppressing the banner until you close your browser.

5. Third-Party Cookies

We may occasionally use third-party services on our website, such as Mapbox, Google Maps, or embedded video players. These providers may set their own cookies on your device to track video playback, map usage, or traffic metrics. Realhouse Estate does not control these third-party cookies; we advise you to review the privacy and cookie policies of those specific providers.

6. How You Can Control Cookies

You have the absolute right to accept or decline cookies. Most web browsers are configured to accept cookies by default, but you can easily modify your browser settings to reject cookies or alert you when a cookie is being sent.

Please note that if you choose to disable cookies or clear your browser's local storage:

  1. Your saved properties in the Wishlist will be permanently cleared.
  2. Our cookie consent banner will reappear on your next visit.
  3. Some advanced search filters or page transitions might not function with optimal responsiveness.

To learn more about how to manage and delete cookies, visit www.aboutcookies.org or www.allaboutcookies.org.

7. Updates to This Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our operational, legal, or regulatory requirements. The date at the top of this policy will be updated accordingly. We encourage you to review this page periodically to stay informed about how we protect your privacy.
