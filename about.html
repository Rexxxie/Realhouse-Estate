Our Story

Nigeria's Most Trusted Property Partner

Founded in Lagos in 2009, LandMark Realty began as a small property consultancy with a bold vision: to make real estate transactions in Nigeria transparent, safe, and accessible to every Nigerian — at home and abroad.

Over 15 years, we have grown into a full-service real estate company with offices in Lagos, Abuja, and Port Harcourt, a dedicated diaspora investment desk, and a team of over 80 certified professionals.

We have facilitated over ₦500 billion in property transactions and helped more than 12,000 families and investors find their perfect properties. Our foundation remains the same: integrity, excellence, and genuine care for every client.
