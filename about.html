Our Story

The UK's Most Trusted Property Partner

Founded in London in 2009, Realhouse Estate began as a small property consultancy with a bold vision: to make real estate transactions in the UK transparent, safe, and accessible to everyone.

Over 15 years, we have grown into a full-service real estate company with offices in London, Edinburgh, and Manchester, a dedicated international investment desk, and a team of over 80 certified professionals.

We have facilitated over £5 billion in property transactions and helped more than 12,000 families and investors find their perfect properties. Our foundation remains the same: integrity, excellence, and genuine care for every client.
